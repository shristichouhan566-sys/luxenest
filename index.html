Ancestral Soul, Contemporary Comfort

The clients inherited a historic property in C-Scheme, Jaipur with breathtaking sandstone arches that had been muffled by claustrophobic partitions and decayed wiring.

The Architectural Challenge

Meticulously reinforcing structural cusped arches without adding weight, while routing modern smart-home automation and HVAC concealed within thick lime walls.

The Modern Rajasthani Solution

Applied traditional Araish mirror lime plaster to reflect courtyard daylight, refinished green Kota stone floors with brass dividing strips, and designed modular teakwood millwork.
