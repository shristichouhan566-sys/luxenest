# LuxeNest — Atelier of Haute Interior Architecture

A bespoke, responsive digital showcase and customer acquisition portal for **LuxeNest**, an award-winning interior architecture brand that harmonizes timeless Indian heritage with avant-garde modern living.

---

## 🏛️ Featured Design Disciplines

1. **Modern Interior**: Geometric clarity, concealed flush doors, flush floor transitions, and razor-sharp architectural cove lighting.
2. **Minimalist Interior**: Wabi-sabi calm, intentional negative space, seamless micro-cement finishes, and organic curved silhouettes.
3. **Luxury Interior**: Book-matched Italian Statuario marble slabs, fluted smoked oak paneling, custom brushed champagne brass joinery, and velvet acoustic panels.
4. **Indian Traditional**: Hand-carved teakwood jharokha niches, hand-beaten brass urlis, Pichwai miniature art alcoves, and block-printed raw silks.
5. **Contemporary**: Sculptural curved bouclé seating, mixed warm metals, statement chandeliers, and gallery-grade art curation.
6. **Bohemian**: Natural cane & rattan webbing, antique Moroccan & Persian kilims, terracotta vessels, and lush biophilic greenery.

## 🕌 Featured Case Study: "The Jaipur Heritage Home"

- **Location**: C-Scheme, Sardar Patel Marg, Jaipur, Rajasthan
- **Carpet Area**: 2,000 sq ft
- **Aesthetic Style**: Modern Rajasthani & Heritage Fusion
- **Total Budget**: ₹8 – 12 Lakhs (Executed at ₹11.2 Lakhs with zero cost overrun)
- **Execution Duration**: 4 Months
- **Interactive Features Included**:
  - **Before / After Renovation Slider**: Draggable visual comparison showing the restored sandstone arches and luminous green Kota stone.
  - **Tactile Material Palette**: Kota Stone, Jaipur Blue Pottery, Araish (mirror lime plaster), Reclaimed Teakwood, and Hand-Beaten Brass.
  - **Budget Allocation Infographic**: Civil (22%), Custom Teak Woodwork (38%), Jali & Lighting (18%), Furnishings & Decor (22%).
  - **Homeowner Testimonial**: Vikramaditya & Gayatri Rathore.

## ⚡ Added Interactive Tools

- **Interactive Interior Cost & Timeline Estimator**: Real-time pricing calculator supporting 1 BHK to 4 BHK / Villa, carpet area slider (600 – 4,500 sq ft), design style multipliers, and material tier modifiers. Outputs live ₹ Lakhs projections and itemized breakdown.
- **AI-Inspired Style Finder Quiz**: 3-question visual discovery quiz matching clients to their ideal design language.
- **Private Consultation Booking Modal**: Seamless modal with validation, property type selector, budget selector, and instant toast confirmation.
- **Turnkey 4-Step Process Roadmap**: Week-by-week timeline from spatial discovery to 3D VR walkthrough and white-glove handover.
- **Responsive Navigation**: Glassmorphic blur header with mobile drawer menu.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Run the included PowerShell HTTP server:

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File D:\luxenest\server.ps1
```

Then open your browser at:
[http://localhost:8088](http://localhost:8088)
