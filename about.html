Our Genesis

Rooted in Jaipur, Crafted for Discerning Homes Across India

Founded in 2014 in the historic pink city of Jaipur, LuxeNest originated as a boutique architectural restoration firm dedicated to conserving Rajasthan's ancestral Havelis. Working side by side with third-generation stone carvers, brass smiths, and Araish lime plasterers, our founders recognized a rare opportunity: to fuse these timeless, organic crafts with modern functional ergonomics and German joinery precision.

Today, LuxeNest has expanded across four flagship design ateliers in Jaipur, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru.
